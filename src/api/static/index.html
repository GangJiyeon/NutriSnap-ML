Detections

Works best with one dish, up close. Every training photo shows a single food, so busy tables with many dishes are a known weak spot.
How it works

From photo to food list

01

Resize like training

The photo is letterboxed to 640×640 with OpenCV, the same way the training images were prepared.

02

Detect with YOLOv8n

A 3.1M-parameter model exported to ONNX scores 8,400 candidate boxes on the CPU.

03

Clean up and return

Overlapping boxes are merged with NMS and scaled back to the original photo, then returned as JSON.

Menu

The 38 foods it knows

Trained on the first bundle of the AI-Hub food image dataset. Anything outside this list will be missed or mistaken for something similar.

Designed and built by Jiyeon Kang Photos are processed in memory and never stored. Trained on the "건강관리를 위한 음식 이미지" dataset from AI-Hub, built with support from the National Information Society Agency (NIA, 한국지능정보사회진흥원) of Korea.
